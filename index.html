05 / METHOD

Built to be
checked.

The Loom treats provenance as part of the interface. Every conclusion keeps a visible path back to the evidence that produced it.

  1. 01

    Observe

    Monitor open cultural sources for new phrases, images, sounds, and formats showing unusual velocity.

    continuous
  2. 02

    Resolve

    Cluster related traces, distinguish copies from mutations, and identify the earliest documented origin.

    source graph
  3. 03

    Forecast

    Estimate lifespan, peak window, migration route, and mutation rate before the lifecycle resolves.

    sealed record
  4. 04

    Publish

    Assemble a readable, cited article alongside the underlying timeline, signals, and confidence.

    living article
  5. 05

    Score

    Resolve the forecast against observed outcomes and retain the result—especially when it was wrong.

    public ledger
